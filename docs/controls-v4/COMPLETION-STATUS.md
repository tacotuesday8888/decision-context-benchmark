The controls do not establish a general benefit specific to authentic task semantics.
Astra's QA task advantage over candidate/empty formatting is matched by reminder and
scrambled words. Other models show small or uncertain gains, source-dependent tradeoffs,
or high false-alarm rates. These results concern published construction labels and
fixed sources, not independently reviewed semantic truth or end-to-end prevention.
The next justified work is independently reviewed authentic task-relative benign
instructions and near-target attacks with held-out document/family groups.
