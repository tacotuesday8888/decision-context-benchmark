## Prioritized findings and remedies

**F1 — High: keep source-label agreement separate from task-relative semantic truth.** The input question asks about authorization, while labels encode source construction. Main tasks/pages come from the released BrowseSafe++ training collection, including privacy rewriting; they are not untouched production requests. `docs/next-study/PROTOCOL.md:18–25`, `docs/study-v7/DATA-PREPARATION.md`, and `scripts/study_v5/prompts.py:3,24–40` establish the distinction. A task-information intervention preserves the original label even when a control removes the task; it does not test the same instruction under two authentic authorizations with independently different labels. Audit cases `browsesafe-v7:08576` and `:24123` expose real boundary questions (ordinary page solicitations versus assistant redirection; unrelated inserted prose versus an instruction). These are flags, not this review's replacement judgments. Remedy: state construction/provenance near the headline, provide a blinded human packet with blank decisions, and retain source labels. Verification: packet covers all 24 flagged pairs and strips labels, model outputs and selection reasons from reviewer views. Human review cannot be claimed complete by generating the packet.

**F2 — Medium: the second-source rate intervals report a seed they did not use.** `analyze_core.py:121,140–144` accepts and records `seed`, then constructs the RNG with module `SEED`; `extension_math.py:73–75` passes the extension seed. This affects provenance for FPR/FNR/BER intervals in three metric cohorts (24 condition/model tables; 72 intervals). Recomputing with the declared extension seed produces **identical endpoints for all 72 saved intervals**; primary contrasts use `bootstrap_mean(..., seed=SEED)` correctly and are unaffected. Remedy: preserve frozen code/results and add a separately versioned correction receipt identifying actual and intended seeds, with an offline replay. No silent replacement or statistical-plan change. Verification must distinguish recorded seed from executed seed and compare every affected interval, not merely tolerate similar numbers.

**F3 — Medium: show the two error types alongside the headline BER effects.** The current primary and follow-up effect plots correctly label percentage points, effect direction, complete-cohort n and finite missingness bounds. However, BER alone conceals Jev's extra missed attacks. Add a presentation-only false-alarm/missed-attack figure from saved same-cohort counts, with one common scale and explicit class denominators. Keep Sol's n=498 separate from native n=2,850; do not depict a matched leaderboard. No new uncertainty estimates are needed for this descriptive count display; link the paired-effect intervals. Do not replace historical figures.

**F4 — Medium: qualify what the controls isolate.** `scripts/study_v5/prompts.py:19–23` repeats “Context unavailable.” to match task character length. It is neither token-matched nor semantically empty: it explicitly signals unavailable context. Generic reminders and empty fields are useful controls, but no single contrast isolates a pure semantic mechanism. The task effect is an effect of this fixed information package. Remedy: name the actual filler and character matching in the overview and claims; retain token/capacity limitations. Do not change frozen prompts. Verify payloads keep each candidate identical across conditions. Main order is hash-interleaved (`study_v7/requests.py:233`), not a constant condition order; the second-source schedule is balanced separately.

**F5 — Medium: make reproduction scope explicit.** Compact follow-up outcomes reproduce the core and second-source analyses exactly in the existing NumPy 2.4.4/SciPy 1.17.1 environment. The v7 full first-attempt rebuild still needs retained ignored evidence, while follow-up compact rows alone cannot prove raw provenance. Existing verification receipts are valuable but do not certify semantic labels or rule out undiscovered defects (F2 illustrates this). Add one offline entry point that checks hashes, independently reconstructs selected means/bounds/counts, reports its raw-sample scope, and never writes to frozen result directories. Human-review generation must also remain local for full source text.

**F6 — Limitation, not a repairable data defect: external validity and dependence.** Grouping closes task/reference/document near relatives and splits before outcomes; no evidence here justifies alleging development leakage. Still, lexical components are not proof of independent semantic families, and public training exposure remains unknown. DTap selects source-observed calls under one published backend and exact witnessed attacks, with predominantly legal tasks. V3 includes reused texts and single-class strata; v4 has only 44 primary clusters. Bootstrap intervals are conditional approximate sampling summaries, not guarantees for all web or agent traffic. Keep these limits; do not retrospectively regroup, relabel or tune thresholds.

**F7 — Limitation: the pilot and stability results cannot carry broader claims.** Keep the zero-attack-success floor, source-visible facts removed by gating, strict-format issue, artificial actions, strong actor instruction and 12-case denominator explicit. Native Luna repeats remain absent. Optional future work could test held-out vulnerable scenarios with validated utility scoring, concurrent route comparisons, token-matched alternatives, or independently adjudicated task-sensitive pairs. None is required just to make the present source-label comparison reportable.

## Strengths and checks against manufactured problems

- Original first failures and exact 0.5 abstentions are retained. All-planned bounds keep known individual errors, and are correctly distinguished from confidence intervals over different complete cohorts.
- The main statistic is a mean of paired group contrasts; clean/attack pages and four conditions are not treated as independent sample-size multipliers. Direct model-effect differences are actually paired, rather than inferred from different significance outcomes.
- V7 has a frozen six-test Holm family. Follow-up intervals are explicitly pointwise/descriptive and do not extend that family. Synthetic calibration of the planned procedure is reported with its limits; no observed-power claim is needed.
- Full candidate text is held fixed; schemas exclude source labels/provenance. Archived native results remain separate from new subscription outcomes. Provider defaults and nonconcurrency are disclosed.
- Source construction, adverse results, operational deferrals and untested prevention are already unusually well documented. Do not demand a new benchmark, a stronger detector or human relabeling of every row merely to report this bounded experiment.
- Visually inspected the v7 primary effect and both follow-up effect figures. No reversed axes or denominator mismatch was found. Different panel scales and planned-versus-complete cohorts are disclosed. F3 is a complementary presentation fix.
